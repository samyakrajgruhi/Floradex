Floradex Achievements & Titles

  Format: Title - Requirement

Collection Achievements

Flower Keeper - Discover 25 flowering plant species. Tree Whisperer -
Discover 25 tree species. Mushroom Hunter - Discover 10 fungi species.
Succulent Collector - Discover 15 succulent or cactus species. Leaf
Collector - Discover 50 unique species.

Exploration Achievements

First Expedition - Scan your first plant. 
Weekend Explorer - Scan plants on 5 different days. 
Trailblazer - Scan 50 unique species. 
Early Bloomer - Make 25 scans before 9:00 AM. 
Night Forager - Make 25 scans after sunset.

Consistency Achievements

Consistent Explorer - Maintain a 7-day scan streak. Nature Devotee -
Maintain a 30-day scan streak. Evergreen - Maintain a 100-day scan
streak. Daily Botanist - Scan at least one new plant for 60 different
days.

Challenging Achievements

Rare Species Hunter - Discover 20 Rare species. Forest Sage - Discover
100 tree species. Bloom Master - Discover 100 flowering plant species.
Master Taxonomist - Discover 100 different genera. Living Library - Read
1000 plant facts. Guardian of Nature - Maintain a 365-day scan streak.
Keeper of the Floradex - Unlock every available achievement.

Future Updates

These achievements depend on features planned for future versions.

Location-Based

World Botanist - Scan plants across 10 different cities or regions.
Regional Explorer - Discover species from 5 different states. Trail
Master - Visit and scan plants from 20 unique parks or botanical
gardens.

Seasonal

Seasonal Explorer - Scan plants during all four seasons. Spring
Botanist - Discover 20 new species during spring. Autumn Wanderer -
Discover 20 new species during autumn.

Weather-Based

Rain Walker - Scan plants during rainfall. Sun Chaser - Scan plants on
an exceptionally hot day. Frost Walker - Scan plants on an exceptionally
cold day.

Event-Based

Earth Guardian - Scan plants on Earth Day. Green Ambassador -
Participate in a Van Mahotsav event. Year One - Use Floradex
continuously for one year.

Biodiversity & Advanced Metadata

Endemic Explorer - Discover 15 endemic species. Conservationist -
Discover 25 endangered species. Habitat Specialist - Discover species
from every supported habitat.
